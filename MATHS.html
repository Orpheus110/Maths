Mathematics in Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence relies heavily on mathematical concepts. Machine Learning models learn patterns through equations, optimization techniques and statistical analysis.

Vectors, matrices and tensors form the backbone of neural networks. Every image, sound and text processed by AI is represented mathematically.

Calculus

Gradient Descent uses derivatives to minimize errors and improve model accuracy during training.

Probability & Statistics

AI systems make predictions using statistical models and probability distributions.
